Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

1. Introduction

Luggas TikTok Notifications provides automated notifications about new public TikTok videos.

2. Data Collection

The service may process publicly available TikTok account information and video links to provide Discord notifications.

3. Data Usage

Information is used solely to detect new public videos and send notifications to the configured Discord channel.

4. Data Sharing

No personal data is sold to third parties. The service uses TikTok and Discord to provide its functionality.

5. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the service administrator through Discord.
